One-line thesis

When a storm, fire, flood, or outage hits, GroundTruth shows utilities exactly what broke, where crews should go first, what equipment they need, what roads are blocked, and what evidence is needed for reimbursement, insurance, regulators, and customers. It is the missing layer between physical reality and operational action — and the company becomes the live truth layer for physical infrastructure.
