The thesis

AI is driving data center electricity demand to roughly double to ~945 TWh by 2030 (~200 GW of capacity). The binding constraint is no longer capital or chips but power and time-to-power. Multi-year grid interconnection queues and a 3–5 year gas-turbine backlog are pushing the industry toward a parallel, behind-the-meter energy system — fuel cells and engines for immediate energization; aeroderivatives and solar-plus-storage for the medium term; heavy-duty gas and nuclear (restarts → SMRs ~2030+) for long-horizon firm power. Winners control a scarce link and compress time-to-power. Use the tabs to explore the data.
